IEEE TSYP14 · NEXUS Technical Challenge

Go-Ma: Stormwater-Drain Overflow Warning

Team of four · Environmental & climate sensing

A low-cost, solar-powered sensor placed inside street drains in Tunisia. It measures how fast the water rises and alerts the municipality and Civil Protection before the drain overflows, from about 15 minutes ahead in a flash flood to over an hour and a half in a slow rise.

ESP32 field nodes read a 4–20 mA level sensor and send data over their own LoRa radio link, with automatic SMS escalation, so the system keeps working when electricity and the Internet fail. Phase 1 report completed; prototype planned for December 2026.

ESP32LoRa4–20 mA sensingSolar powerEarly warning
